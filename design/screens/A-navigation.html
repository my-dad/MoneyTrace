Decisions to confirm

  • Phone: Home keeps its chat composer, which sits directly above the tab bar. Other tabs show only the tab bar.
  • Desktop: the existing mockup (screen 03) had rail items Home, Accounts, Cash, Cards and Settings. Here, Cash and Cards are part of Accounts (screen group C), and the rail uses the same five destinations as the phone. Reminders and Settings sit at the bottom of the rail. The assistant panel from screen 03 stays on Home.
  • Demo marker: a thin strip across the top of every screen, rather than a floating badge. That way it never covers the header buttons or content.
  • Reminder badge counts open follow-ups only; snoozed ones are not counted.
