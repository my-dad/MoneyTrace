Decisions to confirm

  • Amounts: money out is shown plain, money in gets a “+”, and a move between your own accounts (⇄) is neutral. Nothing is red.
  • Failed and rejected items stay in the list with their status, so nothing you pasted silently disappears.
  • Duplicates appear struck through and marked “Not counted twice”, linking to the original.
  • Needs answer sends you to Chat to answer. Activity itself never asks questions.
  • Correct or reverse is one sheet with two modes. A reason is always required, and the original always stays in history.
  • Desktop shows the list with the selected item's detail beside it, instead of a separate page.
