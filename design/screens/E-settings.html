E11 · Privacy

‹Privacy

What MoneyTrace keeps

  • The text of SMS and chat messages you send
  • Your accounts, with only the last 3–4 digits
  • The ledger built from them, with its history of corrections

What it never asks for

  • Bank passwords or PINs
  • One-time codes (OTPs). Messages that look like one are refused and not saved.
  • Full card or account numbers

Who sees it

Only you. Messages the built-in parsers can't read are sent to an AI service to be read; nothing else leaves MoneyTrace.

Decisions to confirm

  • Setup is 5 steps with a progress bar: name, currency and period, accounts (the existing form), opening balances (“starting positions, not income”), reminders. It shows once, for a new user with no accounts.
  • Currency is fixed to BDT, shown but not changeable.
  • AI budget is read-only: today against US$0.05 and the month against US$5.00, with “chat and SMS parsing keep working” when it runs out.
  • Reminders: daily, weekly or off (inbox only), digest time, quiet hours and a minimum amount.
  • Notifications explains the browser permission; the in-app inbox is always on. Notifications never include amounts.
  • Privacy lists what is kept and what is never asked for (passwords, PINs, OTPs, full numbers).
  • Reporting period in Settings opens the same choice as setup step 2.
