H · Sources and chips (CR-008, piece 2)

Each SMS is read for where the money moved: a bank account, a card, bKash, Upay, Nagad or Cash. New references are remembered the first time they appear and shown as chips. Debits count as Spent and credits as Came in, except a debit and a credit of the same amount on two of your sources close together: that is a Transfer, counted in neither. When DeepSeek can't read an SMS clearly, it asks (up to 3 questions).

  2. Transfer rows show a blue Transfer chip plus both sources, and count in neither Spent nor Came in.
  3. Net money replaces "Banks and cash / Cards owed": everything in minus everything out since you started, with a Sources list showing each source's in and out this month.

  5. DeepSeek's questions appear under the pasted SMS with free typing and Skip; at most 3, then "Record it?" with what it has.
