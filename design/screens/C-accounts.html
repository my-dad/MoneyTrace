Decisions to confirm

  • Groups: Bank & wallets, Cash, Held by family, Credit cards. Mobile wallets such as bKash sit with banks.
  • Not yet explained cash appears as its own dashed row inside Cash, with Needs answer, and is counted in your cash total.
  • Cards show the amount owed as a positive number labelled “owed”, never as negative money.
  • Count cash is a 3-step flow: pick the cash and count it, see the difference, then explain it with a required cause or leave it for review. Differences are never auto-categorised as spending.
  • Give cash to family is a custody move, stated as “not spending”.
  • Add account keeps the existing setup form, which moves to its own page.
