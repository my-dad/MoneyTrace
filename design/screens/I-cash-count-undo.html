I · Count your cash, and undo a wrong transfer (release gates)

Two release blockers. Count cash (gate 32): you say how much cash you have, the app compares it with what it expected and shows any gap. It never changes a balance on its own and never calls the gap spending. Not a transfer (CR-008 §9): if two SMS were paired as your own transfer by mistake, one tap counts them separately again (Spent and Came in), through the normal correction trail.

How it behaves

  • Expected cash is your Cash plus cash not yet explained (from withdrawals). Cash held by family is left out, and the question says so.
  • A count is saved every time (as a reconciliation), whatever the result. A gap stays open as a question in the chat until you answer it; it never becomes spending by itself.
  • Adjust needs a typed reason and posts a cash adjustment with that reason and your sign-off (audit). It does not touch Spent.
  • Not a transfer appears on any SMS the app paired as a transfer. It reverses the transfer and records the original debit as Spent and the credit as Came in.
  • Money is checked in whole poisha; the count accepts “2300”, “2,300” or “2300.50”.
