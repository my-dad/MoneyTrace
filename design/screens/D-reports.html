Decisions to confirm

  • Toggle between Calendar month and Salary cycle at the top; arrows step to earlier periods. The cycle start day (the 25th) is set in Settings.
  • Cash not yet explained is its own dashed, neutral line, never part of Spent.
  • Trend is one plain sentence against the same days of the previous period, not a chart.
  • Where it went is one bar per category, largest first, with the amount written beside each bar.
  • Not settled yet lists cash differences, unplaced cash and purchases waiting for an answer, which are left out of the totals until resolved.
  • Before tracking started, a period is empty and says why, rather than showing zeros.
